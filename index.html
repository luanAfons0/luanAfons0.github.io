Questions

Not here? Ask on the call.

Do you work with stores outside Brazil?

Yes. I work remotely, in English or Portuguese.

How do you set the price?

Each package shows a starting price. After a 15-minute call, I send a one-page scope with a fixed price.

I already have a store. Can you help?

Yes. I fix, speed up and add features to stores that already exist. The free audit is a good place to start.

When should I not hire you?

When you need a brand identity or product photos first. I build and fix the store; a designer or a photographer does those better.

Who owns the store and the code?

You do. The accounts, the theme and the code are yours.
