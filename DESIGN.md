# Design System: Luan Afonso, portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Datasheet and the Board"**

The portfolio is one electronic part seen two ways. Outside, it is the part's datasheet: cool paper, black ink, a part number (LA-1) in an inked block, rules under headers, a pin configuration figure, a table of contents with dotted leaders. Inside, it is the board the part sits on: matte-black solder mask, copper traces, white silkscreen print, tinned pads. The Title Screen, Exhibit Panel, and Skip View are reading surfaces and carry both looks through one markup; the game, the Pause Menu, and the resume prompt are always the board.

There are three Themes. Two of them are the machine world: **Paper** is the datasheet; **Board** is the circuit board. The third, **Dungeon**, is its own world (see "Second world" below). A saved Theme sets `data-theme` on the root; with none, `prefers-color-scheme` picks (light gives Paper, dark gives Board). Dungeon has no system trigger: only a saved Theme shows it. Every color on an HTML surface comes from a look token that resolves with `light-dark()`, so one stylesheet serves both. The machine art set (wall textures, Bug frames, posters, sounds, HUD colors) is shared by both Themes and is drawn by a seeded generator, `scripts/machine-art.ts`, not by hand.

The system is dense, flat, and literal. Ornament is taken from real parts and boards, never invented: pin-1 notches, square pads, reference designators, keep-out areas, LED rows, timing diagrams. Copper is scarce and means one thing. Pixel type and pixel art appear only at whole-pixel sizes. State changes are steps, not glows.

**Second world: "The Gamebook and the Keep"**

The Dungeon Theme is a solo adventure gamebook. Outside, the visitor reads an open paperback on its deep green cloth cover: pulp pages, one black ink, running heads, a pen-and-ink plate, stat boxes, and choices set as "New Game ... turn to 1". Each Exhibit is a numbered section, and its Part label is the section number. Inside, the visitor walks a torch-lit keep of blue-grey cut stone, with banners for posters and green Slimes for Bugs (a Slime is still a Bug). The dungeon art set (`scripts/dungeon-art.ts`) draws every texture, Bug frame, banner, page tile, the ink plate, and the sounds, with fixed seeds.

The Dungeon look is printed honestly: one ink, impression, hatch, rules, and boxes. There is no copper and no glow. A live or selected thing is inked: a step inversion to an ink block with page text. Torch orange is only in the game. The direction refuses the gold-serif fantasy skin and the cream parchment with a red seal.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three Themes, one markup: Paper (datasheet) and Board (circuit board) are the machine world, chosen by `data-theme` or the system setting; Dungeon (gamebook) is saved only.
- Archivo (variable width) for reading; Silkscreen for labels, pins, Part labels, and pixel UI.
- Copper marks only the live or selected thing.
- Every Exhibit carries one Part label (U1, J1, R1, Q1) that is the same on the wall poster, in the Exhibit Panel, and in the Skip View.
- Square corners (0 to 2px), hairline and 2 to 3px rules, no gradients (machine world).
- Dungeon: one black ink on pulp, on a green cloth cover; selected means inked; IM Fell English for titles, Alegreya for reading and numerals, Alegreya SC for labels.
- All game art is procedural, seeded, and names its generator as provenance.

## Colors

The machine world is a near-neutral cool palette in two materials, plus one metal: copper. The Dungeon world is one black ink on warm pulp, on a deep green cloth (see "Dungeon" below). Copper is a machine-world color only.

### Primary
- **Copper** (`copper`): Board traces, pads, underlines, lit LEDs, slider thumbs, and "on" switch levers (4.2:1 on the Lifted Mask). On Paper it is never text.
- **Deep Copper** (`copper-deep`): the Paper copper: traces, pins, underlines, and the Paper focus ring, through `--live-trace` and `--focus` (4.6:1 on Datasheet Paper). Also the Paper `::selection` block, with paper text.
- **Hot Copper** (`copper-hot`): copper as text and as the Board focus ring. Used for the selected Board menu label, the Board link text, the open Exhibit's Part label on Board, and the Board "Fault." label (7.1:1 on the Lifted Mask). The HUD values are the same color family, but the HUD keeps its own value (`#e08a4f`, `COPPER_LIT` in `scripts/machine-art/palette.ts`).

### Neutral (Paper: the datasheet)
- **Datasheet Paper** (`paper`): the Paper sheet and panel surface (`--surface`): the Title Screen sheet, the Skip View page column, and the Exhibit Panel. Also the text color on any inked block.
- **Paper Shade** (`paper-2`): the Paper ground around the sheet (`--ground`, 1.17:1 against the sheet), and the fill of the load error note.
- **Black Ink** (`ink`): Paper text, the strong 3px header rule, inked Part label blocks, the chip body, the selected Paper menu item, and Paper buttons.
- **Grey Ink** (`ink-2`): secondary Paper text: header notes, the kind of an Exhibit, footers, pin numbers.
- **Hairline** (`rule`): quiet Paper separators between Exhibits and above the footer (2.0:1 on the sheet).
- **Pin Grey** (`pin`): unselected Paper pins (3.1:1 on the sheet), through `--idle-pad`.

### Neutral (Board: the circuit board)
- **Solder Mask** (`board`): the Board ground (`--ground`): the page under the trace tiles, the wells of the DIP and Theme switches, and the color of the NEW GAME dive.
- **Lifted Mask** (`board-2`): the Board panel and keep-out surface (`--surface`, and `.board-surface--quiet`): the Pause Menu and Exhibit Panel, the chip outline, and the menu keep-out of the Title Screen. It is lighter than the ground (1.13:1, about 6 points of OKLCH lightness), so a panel lifts off the board; the traces of the ground tile stay close to it.
- **Trace Under Mask** (`mask-trace`): the traces in the board tile; never a text color.
- **Silkscreen** (`silk`): Board text and Board headings.
- **Silkscreen Grey** (`silk-2`): secondary Board text, switch legends, outlined Part labels in the Skip View, the "off" lever.
- **Silkscreen Faint** (`silk-3`): Board outlines and separators: the chip keep-out outline, pad traces, panel borders, dashed separators, unlit LEDs. 3.5:1 on the board and 3.1:1 on the Lifted Mask, so an edge reads well above the traces (Trace Under Mask is 1.23:1).
- **Tinned Pad** (`pad`): unselected Board pads, through `--idle-pad`.
- **Scrim** (`scrim`): the dimmer behind overlays, at 62% (Exhibit Panel, Pause Menu) or 50% (resume prompt).

### Machine art set (game only)
The game's colors live in `scripts/machine-art/palette.ts` and `src/game/theme.ts`, not in CSS. They are the same family as the Board tokens.
- **Game Mask** (`game-mask`): the base of every wall texture and poster, and the HUD bar. It carries sparse light and dark specks for a matte look.
- **Game Trace** (`game-trace`): raised copper under the mask on wall textures.
- **Ceiling** and **Floor**: Flats (`heat-sink`, a heat-sink panel grid, and `solder-mask`, solder mask with traces and vias), one per tile, shaded by distance like the walls. `game-ceiling` and `game-floor` are the flat fills the art set keeps until every art set has Flats.
- **Beetle Sheen** (`beetle-sheen`): the one off-palette hue, a muted green on the Bug's dark carapace, so the Bug reads as a living thing and not as board.
- The HUD reuses the Board tokens: labels in Silkscreen Grey, values in Hot Copper, hint and crosshair in Silkscreen, outlines in Silkscreen Faint.

### Dungeon (the gamebook)
Dungeon is one look with no light and dark variants (`color-scheme: light`). The palette tokens are `--dungeon-*` in `src/theme.css`. Under `:root[data-theme="dungeon"]` the look tokens take the Dungeon values: `--surface` is Pulp Page, `--surface-2` is Pulp Shade, `--text`, `--line`, `--live-text`, `--live-trace`, and `--focus` are Black Ink, `--text-2` is Grey Ink, and `--line-2` is Pulp Rule.
- **Cloth Cover** (`dungeon-cover`): the ground around the book, under the weave tile (`.dungeon-cover`). It is the page ground of the Title Screen and the Skip View. The Skip View back link sits on it in Pulp Page text.
- **Cloth Thread** (`dungeon-cover-ink`): the darker thread of the weave tile. Only the generator uses it (`COVER_INK` in `scripts/dungeon-art/palette.ts`), so `src/theme.css` has no token for it.
- **Pulp Page** (`dungeon-page`): every page, panel, slip, and the HUD strip, under the pulp tile (`.dungeon-pulp`). Also the text on any inked block.
- **Pulp Shade** (`dungeon-page-2`): the blocked-way error note, and the light strand of the page leaves.
- **Black Ink** (`dungeon-ink`): all text, every rule and box, tick boxes, the manicule, the selected choice, focus rings, and the `::selection` block. The one ink.
- **Grey Ink** (`dungeon-ink-2`): running heads, kinds, folios, hints ("turn to 1", "back to the keep"), the "Sample content" stamp, the end line, and the HUD labels.
- **Pulp Rule** (`dungeon-rule`): dotted leaders, and the dark strand of the page leaves.
- **Gutter Shade** (`dungeon-gutter-shade`): Grey Ink at 45%, the inset shading where each page of the spread curves into the spine.
- **Leader on Ink** (`dungeon-leader-on-ink`): Pulp Page at 40%, the dotted leader inside the inked (selected) choice.
- **Scrim** (`dungeon-scrim`, 62%): the warm dimmer behind the Pause Menu and the Exhibit Panel. The resume prompt uses the same black at 50% (`dungeon-scrim-resume`).

### Dungeon art set (game only)
The game colors live in `scripts/dungeon-art/palette.ts` and `src/game/theme.ts`, not in CSS.
- **Cut Stone** (`dungeon-stone`) with **Mortar** (`dungeon-mortar`): the blue-grey walls (`stone-a`), with lit top-left and shaded bottom-right edges.
- **Moss** (`dungeon-moss`): the damp wall (`stone-b`).
- **Oak** (`dungeon-oak`): the door planks and the shelf, with wrought iron.
- **Torch Flame** (`dungeon-flame`): the one warm light, only in the torch (with an outer `#c4521f` and a core `#ffd27a`).
- **Slime** (`dungeon-slime`): the Bug body (light `#a6dc78`, shade `#4f9a42`, rim `#2f6b2c`).
- **Ceiling** (`dungeon-game-ceiling`) and **Floor** (`dungeon-game-floor`): flat fills, shaded by distance.
- The HUD and the banners use the book colors: Pulp Page, Black Ink, and Grey Ink.

### Named Rules
**The Copper Means Live Rule.** Copper marks only what is live or selected: the selected menu item and its pin or pad, active links, "on" switches, the slider thumb, lit LEDs, focus rings, signal traces, the open Exhibit's Part label, and the HUD readouts. Everything else is ink, paper, board, or silkscreen.

**The No Copper Ink Rule.** Copper text fails contrast on Paper. On Paper, copper is a trace, pin, underline, or focus ring; the text it marks stays ink. Use the look tokens `--live-text` (text) and `--live-trace` (lines), never raw copper for text.

**The Look Token Rule.** HTML surfaces use the look tokens (`--ground`, `--surface`, `--surface-2`, `--text`, `--text-2`, `--line`, `--line-2`, `--live-text`, `--live-trace`, `--idle-pad`, `--focus`), which resolve per Theme with `light-dark()`. A sheet, panel, or keep-out is `--surface` on `--ground`: in Paper a light sheet on the Paper Shade, in Board the Lifted Mask on the bare board, with a silkscreen edge of 3:1 or more. A surface that is Board in both machine Themes sets `color-scheme: dark` on itself or uses the palette tokens directly.

**The Selected Means Inked Rule.** In Dungeon, a live, selected, hovered, or focused thing is inked: an ink block with Pulp Page text, as one step. There is no copper, no second ink, and no glow. Hover inverts only on a fine pointer (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`), so a tap never leaves a block inked; focus inverts and adds an ink ring.

**The Dungeon Scope Rule.** Every Dungeon rule is under `:root[data-theme="dungeon"]` or on an `.only-dungeon` element. The page grounds come only through `.dungeon-pulp` and `.dungeon-cover`, whose rules name the Dungeon look. So Paper and Board never fetch a Dungeon face, tile, or plate.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (variable, width 62% to 125%, weight 100 to 900), with system-ui fallback
**Body Font:** Archivo
**Label/Mono Font:** Silkscreen (with ui-monospace fallback)

**Dungeon Display Font:** IM Fell English (roman and italic, weight 400 only), with Iowan Old Style and Georgia fallback
**Dungeon Running Head Font:** IM Fell English SC (400)
**Dungeon Body Font:** Alegreya (variable, weight 400 to 900, roman and italic)
**Dungeon Label Font:** Alegreya SC (500 and 700)

**Character:** Archivo, condensed and heavy, is the datasheet's engineering grotesque; Silkscreen is the literal print on the board. Paper sets names and titles in condensed Archivo; Board sets the same names and titles in Silkscreen. Both faces are self-hosted from `public/fonts/` under the OFL.

In Dungeon, IM Fell English is the old letterpress of the gamebook titles; Alegreya reads, and sets every numeral; Alegreya SC sets labels and choices. The four Dungeon faces are self-hosted from `public/fonts/` under the OFL (Latin subsets from Fontsource), as `--font-fell`, `--font-fell-sc`, `--font-book`, and `--font-book-sc`.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Archivo 800, `font-stretch: 72%`, line height 0.88 to 0.9, letter spacing -0.02em): the name "Luan Afonso" on Paper. Title Screen scales it with the sheet (up to 6.08em of the sheet font); the Skip View uses the token size.
- **Display, Board** (Silkscreen 400, snapped down to a multiple of 8px, line height 1): the name on Board.
- **Headline** (Archivo 700 to 800, `font-stretch` 78% to 85%, line height 1 to 1.15): Exhibit titles on Paper. The Exhibit Panel uses 800 at 2.6em of its font.
- **Headline, Board** (Silkscreen 400, 24px in the Skip View, `round(nearest, 2.2em, 8px)` in the Exhibit Panel): Exhibit titles on Board.
- **Title** (Archivo 700, 0.9375rem to 1em, line height 1.2, with a 1px rule under it): datasheet section headings ("Features", "Description", "Links", "Rooms"). ### Named Rules
**The Whole Pixel Rule.** Silkscreen is drawn on an 8px grid. Every Silkscreen size is a whole multiple of 8px, never below 8px. A size that scales is snapped with `max(8px, round(nearest, <size>, 8px))`, or `round(down, ...)` when it must not overflow. A fixed size is written as a multiple of 8px (16px, 24px, 32px).

**The Two Faces Rule.** Archivo reads; Silkscreen labels. Body text, ledes, and long sentences are never Silkscreen. Silkscreen carries names and titles only on Board, where they are print on the board.

**The Lining Figures Rule.** In Dungeon every element sets `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums`, because Alegreya's old-style "1" reads as "I" in "U1". The shared rule in `src/theme.css` is `!important`, so a `font` shorthand cannot reset it. Numerals are Alegreya, never IM Fell English.

**The Book Faces Rule.** IM Fell English sets titles, names, and running heads; Alegreya sets reading text and every number; Alegreya SC sets labels, choices, and key caps. IM Fell English has no bold: never set it above 400.

## Layout

**The 16:9 sheet.** The Title Screen is a 16:9 sheet that fits the window like the game view (`--u: min(1vw, 1.7778vh)`). Its font is `max(14px, 1.25 × --u)`, and every size inside is in em of that font, so the whole sheet scales as one. The Exhibit Panel uses the same idea: its font is `clamp(14px, min(1.25vw, 2.2222vh), 22px)` and its width is 51em (Paper) or 48em (Board).

**Paper layout.** A datasheet: a header with the part number block left and a document note right, on a 3px ink rule; content in two columns (name, description, and Features left; the pin configuration figure, then Table 1 of the controls after a hairline, right); a footer above a hairline. Margins are 3.52em of the sheet. The 16:9 sheet is Datasheet Paper on the Paper Shade ground, which shows where the window is not 16:9.

**Board layout.** The sheet has no fill: the trace tile on Solder Mask runs edge to edge. The name sits inside a chip keep-out outline (Lifted Mask) labeled LA-1; the menu is pads joined by 3px traces to that outline. Columns are 1.15fr and 1fr.

**The Skip View.** One column, max 50rem, 16px side padding. Each Exhibit is a two-column row: a 72px gutter for the Part label and kind, then the text (64px gutter and 16px gap below 35rem). The contents list uses dotted leaders, as a datasheet table of contents.

**The game.** A 640x360 framebuffer, kept at 16:9 and letterboxed in black (`image-rendering: pixelated`). The HUD is a 40px status bar along the bottom, split into outlined sections with 4px margins and 6px padding.

**The Dungeon spread.** The Title Screen sheet becomes a two-page spread, 92 × 50.625 `--u`, on the cloth cover, as a two-column grid. The left page (an `.only-dungeon` element) holds the running head, the ink plate (in a 2px ink frame, 4:3), How to Play, and the folio, with 2.6em 3em 2.2em padding. The right page holds the running head, the name, the description, the stat boxes (three columns, 0.8em gap), the Features lines, the choices, and the foot, with 0 3em 0 3.6em padding. The loading die and the blocked-way note stay in the menu figure in the markup; CSS anchor positioning (`--title-plate`) puts them under the plate in place of How to Play. At an aspect ratio of 1:1 or taller, the spread is one page, right page first, then the plate and How to Play; the leaves, the gutter shading, and the cover shadow drop.

**The Dungeon sheets.** The Pause Menu (the Adventure Sheet) is `min(36em, 100%)` wide with 1.6em 1.8em 1.4em padding; its fields are a two-column grid with a 0.7em gap, and the wide fields span both columns. The Exhibit Panel is `min(46em, 100%)` wide with 1.8em 2.6em 1.5em padding, and keeps a `max(16px, min(2vw, 3.5556vh))` margin from the window. The Skip View is one pulp column, max 46rem, with 40px 48px 32px padding, a 62ch measure, and 26px between sections; below 35rem it goes full width (28px 16px 24px padding) with no back link and no shadow. In the game, the HUD is a 40px pulp strip.

**Spacing.** Fixed spacing is on a 4px base (4, 8, 16, 20, 28, 40px). Overlays keep 16px from the window edge.

### Named Rules
**The Declared Tier Rule.** At narrow sizes, ornament drops in declared steps and content keeps its size. The Title Screen has two tiers: at an aspect ratio of 1:1 or taller it goes to one column, drops the chip outline, and joins the pad traces to a vertical bus; below 600px wide, Paper drops pins 6 to 10 and lists their controls in text instead.

**The Keep-Out Rule.** Board traces never run under text. Text sits on a keep-out (a solid `--surface` area, the Lifted Mask in Board, with a silkscreen edge) or on the quiet board tile, whose traces are close to the mask color. The busy tile is for open background only.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from material and from overlays that sit over the paused game, not from layered cards. Only the two pause-time overlays (Exhibit Panel, Pause Menu) cast a shadow, a broad soft drop that lifts the sheet off the dimmed game. The resume prompt has none. Nothing on a page casts a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Sheet over game, Paper** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 50px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55)`): the Exhibit Panel in Paper.
- **Sheet over game, Board** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 40px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): the Exhibit Panel in Board and the Pause Menu.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Page Rule.** In the machine world, pages are flat. A shadow belongs only to an overlay that pauses the game.

### Dungeon depth
In Dungeon the page is paper on a cloth cover, so depth is the depth of a book. The spread and the Skip View sheet cast a shadow tinted with the cover's dark green. The pages darken into the spine, and the stacked leaves show at each outer edge. The overlays keep the machine sheet shadows.
- **Book on cloth** (`box-shadow: 0 1.2em 2.6em rgb(6 18 11 / 0.55), 0 0.2em 0.4em rgb(6 18 11 / 0.4)`): the Title Screen spread.
- **Sheet on cloth** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgb(6 18 11 / 0.5)`): the Skip View sheet, above 35rem only.
- **Gutter** (`box-shadow: inset ±2.2em 0 2em -1.6em`, in Gutter Shade): each page, at the spine side.
- **Sheet over game**: the Exhibit Panel uses the Paper value (`0 18px 50px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55)`); the Pause Menu keeps the Board value. The resume slip has none.

**The Book on Cloth Rule.** In Dungeon, a page lies on the cover, so it may cast a shadow in the cover's dark (`rgb(6 18 11)`). Nothing on a page casts a shadow; boxes, stat boxes, and choices are flat ink.

## Shapes

Corners are square. The only radii are 1px on pads, pins, and switch thumbs, and 2px on the chip body and key caps. The one curve is the pin-1 notch on the chip body (a half circle). Lines come in three weights: 1px hairlines and separators, 2px outlines (Board panels, Board Part labels, the chip keep-out, focus rings), and 3px strong rules (the Paper header rule, copper and silkscreen traces). Pause Menu separators are 1px dashed. List markers are small square pads, not bullets. Pixel icons are drawn as SVG on an 8x8 grid with `shape-rendering: crispEdges`.

**Dungeon.** Corners are square, as in print. The 2px radius is only on key caps and the loading die. The only circles are the die pips and the Theme radio boxes of the Adventure Sheet. Lines are ink: 1px for running-head rules, field boxes, the panel foot, and the strands of a double rule; 2px for the plate frame, stat boxes, choices, sheet items, Close, the resume slip, and tick boxes. A double rule is two 1px lines 2px apart (around the section number), or a 2px border with a 1px ink outline inset 0.45em (the Adventure Sheet) or 0.4em (the resume slip). Leaders are 2px dotted Pulp Rule. A list marker is an empty 0.5em tick box. A fixed Bug is a box with an ink X. The pointing hand (manicule) is an SVG symbol, not a glyph. The page leaves are 0.5em strips of 1px lines in Pulp Shade and Pulp Rule.

## Components

### Part label
The reference designator of an Exhibit (U project, J contact, R skill, Q job). The same label appears in silkscreen on the wall poster, in the Exhibit Panel, and in the Skip View.
- **Paper:** an inked block, paper text, Silkscreen label size.
- **Board, Skip View:** a 2px Silkscreen Grey outline, Silkscreen text.
- **Board, Exhibit Panel (open Exhibit):** a 2px copper outline with Hot Copper text; the letter encodes the kind, so no kind label shows.
- **Poster:** the label in silkscreen above the component, a copper outline around it; the component shows the kind (IC, connector, resistor, transistor).

### Title Screen menu (pins and pads)
- **Paper:** menu items are pin labels of a 6-pin chip; pins 1 to 3 are the menu (NEW GAME, SKIP, Theme), pins 4 to 6 are NC. The selected item is an inked block; its pin turns copper and a 3px copper trace joins label to pin. The controls are Table 1, under the figure: key and action twice per row, a 2px ink rule over the head, hairlines between rows, the actions in Silkscreen.
- **Theme picker (open select):** an attached dropdown under the select, its left edge on the select's, at least as wide, one option per row. Paper: Datasheet Paper, 1px ink border, the active row an ink block with paper text. Board: Lifted Mask, 2px Silkscreen Grey edge, a pad on every row, the active row Hot Copper with a copper pad. Dungeon: a pulp slip with a double ink rule, the active row inked. The current Theme has a check at the right of its row: a 7×5 pixel check in Paper and Board, an ink tick in Dungeon.
- **Board:** each item is a pad (2.4em × 1.6em, 1px radius, Tinned Pad) with a 3px silkscreen trace to the chip outline. The selected pad and its trace turn copper; the label turns Hot Copper.
- **Focus:** 2px `--focus` outline, 4px offset. **Press:** 1px down.
- **NEW GAME dive:** a Solder Mask layer grows from the chip (Paper) or the selected pad (Board) to the full window in 420ms (`cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`). Reduced motion skips it.

### Loading and errors
- **Paper loading:** a timing diagram; the CLK wave marches in steps (`steps(2)`, 0.5s).

### Buttons

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the look tokens on HTML surfaces and let `light-dark()` pick the Theme; keep the saved-Theme block and the system-setting block of a surface identical.
- **Do** give every Exhibit one Part label and show the same label on the poster, the Exhibit Panel, and the Skip View.
- **Do** snap every Silkscreen size to a multiple of 8px, with `round()` when the size scales.
- **Do** make select, press, and fix step changes: a color swap, a 1px press, frame steps (`steps()`), a "patched" mark.
- **Do** put text on a keep-out or on the quiet board tile, never on the busy trace tile.
- **Do** draw new game art in `scripts/machine-art/` with a fixed seed, and let the file name the generator.
- **Do** take ornament from real datasheets and boards: pin numbers, notches, pads, designators, dotted leaders, LEDs, timing diagrams.
- **Do** write every Dungeon rule under `:root[data-theme="dungeon"]` or on an `.only-dungeon` element, and put the page grounds on with `.dungeon-pulp` and `.dungeon-cover`.
- **Do** ink the selected thing in Dungeon: an ink block with Pulp Page text, as one step; invert on hover only on a fine pointer.
- **Do** set every numeral in Alegreya in Dungeon; the shared lining figures rule covers every `font` shorthand.
- **Do** take Dungeon ornament from real gamebooks and printed books: running heads, folios, section numbers between double rules, stat boxes, tick boxes, dotted leaders, the manicule, the pen-and-ink plate.
- **Do** draw new Dungeon art in `scripts/dungeon-art/` with a fixed seed, and let the file name `scripts/dungeon-art.ts`.
- **Do** call a Slime a Bug in every label and text.

### Don't:
- **Don't** set copper text on Paper; use an ink label with a copper underline or trace.
- **Don't** use copper for anything that is not live or selected.
- **Don't** use soft glows, blurs, or gradients for state. (The Dungeon tick-box X is drawn with hard-stop gradients; it is a mark, not a blend.)
- **Don't** set body text or long sentences in Silkscreen.
- **Don't** round corners past 2px. (In Dungeon, only the die pips and the Theme radio boxes are round.)
- **Don't** shrink content at narrow sizes; drop ornament in declared steps instead.
- **Don't** add shadows to anything on a page; only overlays over the paused game cast one. (In Dungeon, a page on the cloth casts one too.)
- **Don't** use copper, a second ink color, or a glow in Dungeon. Torch orange is only in the game.
- **Don't** set a numeral or a bold in IM Fell English.
- **Don't** make Dungeon a gold-serif fantasy skin or cream parchment with a red seal.
- **Don't** let Paper or Board fetch a Dungeon face, tile, or plate.
