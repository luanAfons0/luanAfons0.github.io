# Award-winning frontend portfolios for motion inspiration

Date: 2026-10-19

Question: Which personal portfolios of frontend and creative developers inspire animation and motion, and have awards you can check on the award site?

## Method and limits

- Award claims come from the Awwwards page of each site (`awwwards.com/sites/<slug>`).
- "Tech tags" are the tags on the Awwwards page. The submitter picks them. They are **not** proof of the code. Only Bruno Simon has a tech claim checked on the author's own case study and source.
- FWA and CSSDA pages could not be fetched (FWA search returned empty, CSSDA search found no entry). FWA and CSSDA counts below come from the owner's own site and are marked "self-claim".
- Awwwards scores shown are from each page. The Dev Award "Animations" sub-score is useful to compare motion quality.

## Ideas relevant to a raycaster / game portfolio

1. **The site is a game.** Bruno Simon turns navigation into play (drive a car, 3D menu models, achievements). A raycaster can use the same idea: rooms for projects, doors for sections. Source: [entry 1](https://www.awwwards.com/brunos-portfolio-case-study.html).
2. **Shared world state.** His weather and leaderboard are the same for all visitors. A raycaster could share a daily high score or a visitor counter. Source: [entry 1](https://www.awwwards.com/brunos-portfolio-case-study.html).
3. **Performance first, with a mobile preset.** He cut effects on mobile; "if the experience runs at 10 FPS, no one will stick around." Add a low-quality preset (fewer rays, lower resolution). Source: [entry 1](https://www.awwwards.com/brunos-portfolio-case-study.html).
4. **Support keyboard, touch and gamepad.** Same source.
5. **Sound as design.** Spatial audio in Bruno's site; sound toggle and rhythm in [Maria Vasilyeva](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/maria-vasilyeva-portfolio) and [Pacôme Pertant](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/pacome-pertant-portfolio). Add a mute toggle and footstep or door sounds.
6. **Retro minimal style.** [Wodniack](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/aw-portfolio) won with a retro, minimal look (tags: Retro, Experimental). A Doom look can win too.
7. **Preloader and transitions as motion.** [Elliott Mangham](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/elliott-mangham) (preloader, Vite + GSAP) and [Gradogna](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/gianluca-gradogna-portfolio) (page transition, clip animation). Use a Doom-style screen wipe between sections.
8. **Judges score motion and also semantics, accessibility and WPO.** Every Dev Award page shows these sub-scores; accessibility is often the lowest (6.2 to 7.4). A raycaster needs a plain HTML fallback and a reduced-motion path to score well. Source: any Dev Award page above, e.g. [Jesper Landberg](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/jesper-landberg-4).
