## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a solo adventure gamebook: you read the opening section, choose where to turn, and walk the dungeon it describes. Refuses the gold-serif fantasy skin and the cream-parchment-plus-red-seal default.
OWN-WORLD: An open paperback on a deep green cover (#1d4a33, the cover owns the page ground); pulp pages #e0d9c5 with black ink #171717; one ink, printed honestly (impression, hatch, rules, boxes), no glows. Selected means inked: a step inversion to an ink block with page text. Part labels head each section as the gamebook section number. IM Fell for titles and numbers, Alegreya for reading, Alegreya SC for labels. Inside: torch-lit blue-grey stone, green Slime Bugs, torch orange only in the game.
STORY: The visitor opens the book on Luan Afonso, reads the Adventure Sheet stats (Rooms, Exhibits, Bugs), chooses NEW GAME, walks the stone keep, reads Exhibits as numbered sections, clears Slimes, and leaves with a contact link.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A two-page spread on green: left page a full pen-and-ink plate of the dungeon corridor with a Slime; right page the running head, the name at display size, the background paragraph, the Adventure Sheet stat boxes, then choices set as "New Game ... turn to 1" with the number in a box; the selected choice is inked.
FORM: The Gamebook, grounded list position 7 (assigned); Adventure Module, position 1 (pick, not shown). Seed key 69160f16. Raises: honest single-ink print (from the expedition ice press); marks accumulate, each cleared Slime ticks a box on the Adventure Sheet (from the passport).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
