Scope: whole app re-theme "Inside the machine" (Title Screen, game frame + HUD, Pause Menu, Exhibit Panel, resume prompt, Skip View, sounds). Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters and developers on desktop. Content: sample Exhibits, marked as samples. Unresolved: A or B is chosen in the mock.

## Direction contract

THESIS: You read the part, then walk inside it. Refuses the dark terminal skin with one neon accent and the Doom brown-red menu.
OWN-WORLD: A (Datasheet and Board): cool paper #f2f3f0 with black ink for reading surfaces (Title Screen, Exhibit Panel, Skip View); matte-black solder mask #0f1214, copper #c06a36, white silkscreen for the board (game, Pause Menu). B (Circuit Board): board materials everywhere. Copper marks only live or selected things. Silkscreen pixel labels at whole-pixel sizes; reference designators (U1, J1, R1, Q1) per Exhibit.
STORY: The visitor sees a part named LA-1 Luan Afonso, picks a pin, enters the board, finds Exhibits as labeled components, fixes insect Bugs, and leaves with a contact link.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A: datasheet cover, part number and name top-left at display scale, chip pinout right with NEW GAME and SKIP as pins, the selected pin's trace in copper. B: top-down board, name in silkscreen, pads joined by traces as the menu.
FORM: Datasheet and Board, grounded list position 4 (assigned); Circuit Board, position 1 (pick). Seed key 8872a8ac.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
