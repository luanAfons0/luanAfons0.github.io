# Freelance home (surface brief)

Scope: the freelance home page for Luan Afonso, Shopify developer. Mode: Persuade. WooCommerce and the game are out (user decisions, 2026-10-20). Replacement world chosen 2026-10-20: The Store Audit (the Till Receipt world is retired; kept for comparison at .impeccable/mocks/ui-studio/freelance-home-till.html).

Audience and job: owners of small and mid-size stores, Brazil and international. Trust Luan fast, see packages and from-prices, then book a call (WhatsApp second). Proof on hand: no client work yet; demo stores, demo metrics and audits are labelled demo. Prices are placeholders Luan sets. Copy in English and pt-BR with a switch; BRL and USD. Light only (user choice). Full infinite virtual scroll stays (user choice). Vanilla TS + Vite later, 100 KB gzipped JS, keyboard, reduced motion.

Pinned by the user: the shopify.design first screen (huge tight grotesk headline on white, hairline rule with small labels, pill buttons with an arrow, rounded media tiles) and mini store UI on the carousel cards.

Memorable moment: scrolling steps a load filmstrip of real mini store screens; each frame settles whole into the center of a fan while its timestamp lights on the rule.

Unresolved: real prices, real demo stores, Luan's photo, WhatsApp number, booking link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page reads as the audit Luan would send a store owner: a store measured frame by frame, then fixed, priced and booked. It refuses the dark freelancer template and the cream editorial portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white ground, near-black ink, hairline report rules, one pass-green accent that burns only on the live element (the active frame, the key line, focus). One tight grotesk at display and body sizes; a mono only for measured data (timestamps, metrics). Rounded 20px media tiles holding real mini store components; black pill actions with an arrow; state shown as marks (check, tick, flag), never a new hue.

STORY: The visitor reads the offer in one line, watches a demo store load through four frames (hello, product and cart, speed score, booking), sees from-prices as a report table, assembles a project strip whose total rolls, sees the process and labelled demos, and books a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered two-line headline at about 7rem, one-line subtext, black pill Book a call plus WhatsApp text link; a hairline rule with a mono label at each end and four timestamp ticks; under it the filmstrip fan of portrait store frames, cut by the bottom edge.

FORM: The Store Audit, position 5 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key 130293c9. Raises: frames settle whole and state is a mark (cutting bench); a hairline page map under the nav replaces the scrollbar (sleeping city); the builder locks items into one project strip (Breton ornament); one accent marks the line that matters per section (zine); one center axis for headline and fan (Versailles); accent only on the live element (neo-tokyo).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
